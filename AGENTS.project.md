# MatMap — product contract

BJJ personal training log. Not a social network.

## Pages (only these)

Today · Log · Map (system) · Cards · Me  
Plus: session composer, session detail, card detail.

No feed, rankings, coach dashboard, or extra tabs.

## Recording (the one flow that must stay fast)

Open Today → start. Mix voice (20s), chips, whiteboard photo.  
Save a Session with many Cards.  
Chips: position → action (tapping an action adds a card). Result defaults to drill.

## Names

Display technique names in **English**. Keep zh/en/id aliases for voice and search so the graph does not fragment.

## Graph

Official ~40-node catalog. Practiced = solid edges. Suggested = dashed (tap to accept). Unpracticed nodes are pale. This is the athlete’s statistical subgraph, not a world map.

## Look

Koryu densho: washi, seigaiha, sumi, vermillion hanko, Zen Antique + Zen Kaku Gothic New.

## Platform

iOS-first (~402×874). Auth on. Per-user data.  
Free: chips, graph, cards.  
Pro: voice, AI diagrams, next-day review, PDF.

## Lead rule

One high-impact change per session. Do not gold-plate.
